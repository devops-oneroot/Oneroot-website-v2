Technology

Software and sensors, built for how farming really works.

The tools farming needed did not exist, so we built them. Our own team writes the apps, trains the cameras and turns field data into advice.

01

Markhet

The problem
Buyers and farmers rarely meet. Every layer in between takes a cut and hides where the produce came from.
What we built
A marketplace app where wholesalers, retailers and processors buy directly from farmers, with every lot traceable to its farm.
Why it matters
Farmers get a fairer price, and buyers know exactly what they are getting.
Markhet on Google Play
02

Markhet Farmer

The problem
A farmer often learns the real price only after the sale.
What we built
An app for farmers to see buyers and prices, and book a sale from their phone.
Why it matters
The farmer decides when and to whom to sell, with the price in front of them.
Markhet Farmer on Google Play
03

Tender coconut counting

The problem
Counting thousands of coconuts by hand is slow, and the count is the most common argument at the point of sale.
What we built
A computer vision system that counts tender coconuts from camera images at our collection centres.
Why it matters
One count both sides can trust, done in a fraction of the time.
Under developmentMap coming soon
04

Satellite field advice

The problem
Expert advice is out of reach for most small farms.
What we built
Satellite data on NPK, pH, EC and moisture, turned into plain advice for each field, at no cost to the farmer.
Why it matters
Farmers act on what their field needs, before problems show up.
05

Soil testing lab

The problem
Farmers buy fertiliser without knowing what their soil lacks.
What we built
A lab inside the store, running both chemical (wet) tests and spectrometer tests.
Why it matters
Advice and purchases start from the soil report, not from guesswork.
