Case study Tender coconut

What happens when one buyer pays for quality.

30,000coconuts bought directly from farmers every day
2xnearly, the rise in district prices
What we did

We bought tender coconut straight from farmers and paid a better price for good nuts.

The pushback

Traders objected. We held our price. Their margin did not change, so the gain went to the farmer.

The result

Prices across the district nearly doubled, for every farmer, not only those who sold to us.
