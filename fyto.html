The crop program

One bottle for each stage of the crop.

Instead of a shelf of separate products, the farmer gets one bottle per growth stage, labelled with the days it is for. It holds what the crop needs at that point and nothing else.

01Made for one crop

Each line is built around a single crop, so every product is aimed at that crop's problems.

02Timed to the stage

Bottles are labelled by day range, like 30 to 60 days, so the farmer knows exactly when to use each one.

03Start at any stage

No lock-in. A farmer can join mid-season or stop whenever they want.
